FlightWay Commons

This isn't built yet, and there's no launch date. Commons is an idea for a peer-advice space — we're putting it here early so the people who'd actually use it can tell us if we're building the right thing.

What it will be

Three ideas we're building toward, plus one thing it will not be.

Grouped by school and by career

Advice threads organized around the two things that actually change what someone should tell you — where you go and where you're headed.

A step or two ahead, not a lifetime

Answers from students who were in your position one or two years ago and actually remember what the question felt like.

Moderated by default

Real names are optional, but every space is moderated from day one — not bolted on after it becomes a problem.

What it deliberately won't be: not a job board, not anonymous by default, and not a replacement for your career center — it's peer advice, not professional advice.
