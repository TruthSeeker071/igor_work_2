How we calculate fit

Overall fit is 75% personality cosine fit plus 25% objective cosine fit (when you have academics or resume signals). With personality only, overall fit equals personality fit.

Personality fit compares your work-style vector to each career’s O*NET profile. Objective fit compares your skills and academics vector to career requirements.

Sector tile color and orb glow follow the overall fit rarity scale (common through mythic). Inside a sector, the same scale applies to each career orb.
