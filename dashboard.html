How we calculate fit

Overall fit blends how a career suits the way you like to work (75%) with what you already bring — your skills, classes, and experience (25%, once we know about them). Until then, it's based on your work style alone.

Personality fit compares how you like to work against what each career actually involves, using real occupational data. Objective fit compares what you've built so far against what the career asks for.

Each dot is a career, colored by its industry. The brighter and bolder a dot, the stronger your fit — weaker matches fade to gray. Move your cursor over the map to light up the careers near it. It's a starting read, not a final answer: it sharpens as we learn more about you.
