What a pilot looks like

Pilots are small and hands-on right now — we're working directly with a handful of offices, not running a self-serve funnel.

1

A scoping call

Thirty minutes to understand your office, your students, and what a useful pilot would actually look like for you.

2

A cohort link and a read-out

Your students take the quiz through one link. We walk your team through what the cohort's matches actually look like — your real students, not a demo.

3

A 6–8 week run, then a debrief

The cohort uses FlightWay for six to eight weeks. At the end you get a written debrief: what we saw, what worked, and whether it's worth continuing.

Questions career centers ask

Short, honest answers.

What happens to student data?

The same thing that happens for any FlightWay account: quiz answers, fit scores and the plan a student builds are stored under our privacy policy, and a student can delete their own account and data at any time. A pilot does not give your office access to any individual student's account.

Does FlightWay replace our career center?

No, and it is not built to. FlightWay covers breadth and week-to-week follow-through. Your office holds what no dataset has — which employers recruit here, and which alumni are reachable.

What does it cost our students?

Nothing changes for them. The quiz, the matches and the weekly plan stay free for individual students whether or not your institution runs a pilot.

Do you integrate with our LMS or Handshake?

Not today. A pilot runs on a single cohort link students open in a browser — there is no LMS, SSO or Handshake integration yet.

What's the time commitment for our office?

A scoping call, a cohort link with a shared read-out of the results, and a 6 to 8 week run that ends in a written debrief. Pilots are small and hands-on right now, not a self-serve product.

More on data and deletion in our privacy policy. General questions go to contact.
